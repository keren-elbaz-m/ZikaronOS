## Summary

The public "I want to tell" witness intake. Legacy is broken: stub steps, hard-coded 2024 dates, availability never sent, and every submit PATCHes witness id 4. The first decision is **rebuild or drop**. The plan covers the rebuild: a public form that creates an unverified witness lead, with availability on the real salon days, for staff to verify in the admin.
